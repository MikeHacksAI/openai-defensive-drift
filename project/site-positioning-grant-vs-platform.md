# Defensive Drift Website Positioning — Grant Research vs Platform Vision

## Decision

The broader Defensive Drift detection-engineering roadmap should strengthen the public project story without expanding the active grant critical path.

The current OpenAI Cybersecurity Grant research program remains the immediate priority. Grant M2 through M5 continue on their existing sequence. Platform roadmap milestones `DD-M1` through `DD-M10` are a longer-term architecture and backlog, not a new requirement to complete before the grant package.

## Public positioning

The website should keep the current measurable research question prominent:

> Can AI reliably distinguish a new security problem from a duplicate, recurrence, related-but-distinct condition, already-remediated condition, or case with insufficient evidence?

The broader platform vision may be introduced around that question rather than replacing it.

Recommended overall framing:

> Defensive Drift is an evidence-preserving AI behavioral detection-engineering research project. Its current grant research phase is building a reproducible benchmark and evaluation pipeline to measure whether AI can safely reconcile new, duplicate, recurrent, related, and insufficiently evidenced security observations.

This lets the site explain both:

1. **what is being proven now** — the benchmark and evaluation research; and
2. **what that research is intended to enable later** — a broader evidence-preserving detection-engineering platform with versioned rules, historical replay, provenance, reconciliation, auditability, and observability.

## Scope guardrail

Do not interrupt Grant M2 to build the entire long-term platform.

Near-term roadmap capabilities should be pulled forward only when they directly improve scientific validity, provenance, reproducibility, reviewer confidence, or reduce rework in the active grant sprint.

The website must not imply that unbuilt `DD-*` capabilities already exist or that all ten platform milestones are grant deliverables.

## Site-change rule

Any public-site wording change should be controlled and modest:

- preserve the active grant research question;
- preserve the M1–M5 grant roadmap as the current execution path;
- optionally add a clearly labeled long-term platform vision;
- keep performance claims gated on measured benchmark results;
- distinguish current capabilities from planned architecture.
